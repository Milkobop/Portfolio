Probably Human

About

Probably Human is a social deduction game set at airport security. Your job is simple: don't let the aliens through. Inspect passengers, check their documents, and make the call — human or not? One wrong decision and the invasion begins.

Project Info

  • Role: Gameplay Programmer
  • Team Size: 4
  • Duration: 1 Month
  • Engine: Unity (C#)

Introduction

Probably Human was a team project built over one month, inspired by games like Papers Please. Players take on the role of an airport security officer tasked with identifying alien infiltrators disguised as humans. Each passenger presents subtle (and not-so-subtle) clues — it's up to the player to decide who gets through and who gets flagged.

What I Learned

Collaborating in a team of four with clearly defined roles and responsibilities. Implementing passenger behaviour logic and decision systems in Unity. Designing game mechanics that balance challenge and clarity for the player. Gained experience in rapid iteration and team communication under a one-month deadline.
