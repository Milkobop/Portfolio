Catpuccino

About

Catpuccino is a cosy café management game where you build the purr-fect café, one Catpuccino at a time. Take orders from adorable cat customers, craft drinks, and keep your café running smoothly. Cute, calm, and full of charm.

Project Info

  • Role: Gameplay Programmer
  • Team Size: 5
  • Duration: 2 Months
  • Engine: Unity (C#)

Introduction

Catpuccino was built by a team of five over two months, blending café simulation with cute cat theming. Players manage a growing café, taking orders, preparing drinks, and satisfying increasingly demanding feline customers. The game was designed to feel relaxing and rewarding, with a strong focus on visual polish and satisfying feedback loops.

What I Learned

Implementing order and queue management systems in Unity. Working within a five-person team with artists, designers, and other programmers to deliver a cohesive product. Focusing on game feel — ensuring interactions were satisfying and responsive. Gained experience integrating UI systems with gameplay logic and managing scope within a two-month timeline.
