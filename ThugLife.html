Thug Life

About

Thug Life is a game made in 48 hours for Global Game Jam 2026. Built with a team of four, it's a fast-paced, chaotic experience created under the pressure of a weekend deadline. Expect big ideas, quick decisions, and a whole lot of fun.

Project Info

  • Role: Gameplay Programmer
  • Team Size: 4
  • Duration: 48 Hours (Global Game Jam 2026)
  • Engine: Unity (C#)

Introduction

Thug Life was our entry for Global Game Jam 2026. With only 48 hours on the clock, the team had to move fast — concepting, prototyping, and shipping a complete game from scratch. The jam environment pushes you to prioritise ruthlessly and trust your instincts as a developer.

What I Learned

Thriving under extreme time pressure and delivering a finished product in 48 hours. Coordinating quickly with a team of four to divide tasks and avoid bottlenecks. Making fast, confident design and technical decisions without overthinking. This jam reinforced how much can be achieved with focus, communication, and a clear shared vision.
