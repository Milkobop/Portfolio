Little Boat

About

Little Boat is a charming puzzle game where you guide a tiny origami boat across a tranquil pond. Navigate obstacles, catch the current, and find your way to the other side. Built in 48 hours for a game jam, Little Boat is all about simplicity and charm.

Project Info

  • Role: Gameplay Programmer
  • Team Size: 4
  • Duration: 2 Days (Game Jam)
  • Engine: Unity (C#)

Introduction

Little Boat was created during a game jam with a team of four. The brief was to make something small, focused, and fun — and we leaned into the theme of fragility and navigation. The origami aesthetic was chosen to complement the delicate feel of guiding something so small across something so vast.

What I Learned

Developing a complete, polished game within a strict 48-hour deadline. Rapid prototyping and scope management — knowing what to cut and what to keep. Collaborating efficiently with a small team under pressure. Implementing simple but satisfying boat movement and water interaction mechanics in Unity.
