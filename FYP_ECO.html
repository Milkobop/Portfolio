FYP ECO

About

ECO is an ecosystem simulation built as my Final Year Project. Wolves hunt deer, deer graze on grass — each population dynamically rises and falls based on the others. The simulation explores emergent behaviour and predator-prey relationships in a living, breathing virtual world.

Project Info

  • Role: Sole Developer
  • Team Size: 1
  • Duration: 7 Months
  • Engine: Unity (C#)

Introduction

ECO was developed as my university Final Year Project, giving me the freedom to explore a topic I find genuinely fascinating: emergent systems and artificial life. The project simulates a simple food chain — grass, deer, and wolves — where each agent makes autonomous decisions using steering behaviours and state machines, resulting in an ecosystem that evolves without any scripted outcomes.

What I Learned

Designing and implementing autonomous agent AI using steering behaviours (seek, flee, wander) and finite state machines. Managing population balance in a dynamic simulation where small tuning changes have large emergent effects. Structuring a solo long-form project over 7 months, including research, prototyping, iteration, and documentation. Deepened my understanding of Unity's physics, spatial partitioning for performance, and data-driven design.
