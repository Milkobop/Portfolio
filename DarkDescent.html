Dark Descent

About

Dark Descent is a 2D Unity platformer where you play as Lily, navigating a treacherous cave teeming with monsters. To escape, you must defeat enemies and search for hidden keys. Although the prototype is complete, additional levels, a more robust storyline, and a defined ending are in development.

Project Info

  • Role: Gameplay Programmer
  • Team Size: 1
  • 6 Weeks
  • Engine: Unity, using C#

Introduction

Dark Descent was created to explore 2D platforming mechanics with a focus on level design and enemy AI. The prototype emphasizes tight controls, challenging puzzles, and a suspenseful atmosphere. For more technical details, refer to my GitHub and itch.io pages.

What I Learned

Through Dark Descent, I refined my skills in Unity’s 2D tools and C#. I learned how to create responsive character controls, implement enemy pathfinding, and design engaging levels. The experience also highlighted the importance of playtesting and iterative design.
