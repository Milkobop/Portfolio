Hi, I'm Shannon! I'm a Games Designer and Gameplay Programmer with a First-Class Honours degree in Games Design and Development (Programming) from the University of Greenwich.

I specialise in bridging creative game design and technical development, bringing ideas to life through C#, C++, Unreal Engine Blueprints and Unity. I particularly enjoy building gameplay systems, AI behaviours and interactive mechanics that make games feel great to play.

I'm currently a Games Technician at London College of Contemporary Arts (LCCA), where I support aspiring developers, deliver technical workshops and help students turn their ideas into playable experiences. I also work in matchday IT support at Arsenal Football Club, drawing on my background in technical troubleshooting and problem-solving.

I love learning new technologies, collaborating with creative people and tackling tricky programming challenges. Whether I'm creating a playful experience or refining a complex system, I aim to bring thoughtful design and clean, readable code together.
